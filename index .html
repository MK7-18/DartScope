Calibration modesAuto: finds the red and green rings in the picture, fits an ellipse for the board plane, then you tap the 20 segment once. Fast but approximate (it ignores strong perspective). Needs good light, the whole board in frame and no other red or green objects. Always check the overlay.
Manual: tap the four reference wires on the outer double ring (20|1, 6|10, 3|19, 11|14), then the bull. Each tap opens a fine-adjust step: use arrow keys or on-screen arrows, then Confirm point. This builds a perspective mapping and reports the bull error in mm.
Recalibrate whenever the phone or board moves.
